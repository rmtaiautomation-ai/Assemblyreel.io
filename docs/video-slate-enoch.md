# Enoch channel — first five videos

Five episodes to test the pipeline with. Every claim below was checked against the text of
1 Enoch in August 2026; none of it is invented, and each one maps onto facts already
verified in the channel's ledger.

Deliberately chosen from passages the reference channel has **not** covered. Its catalogue
already holds the ocean floor, hell, heaven, Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, the Moon, the cremated,
the stars, life on other planets, seven levels beneath the earth, the Grand Canyon, Jesus,
and what God made before humans. Competing against a 1.7M-view video on its own subject is
the worst possible first test.

---

## What the title formula actually is

From fifteen titles with view counts, the shape is fixed:

> **The Book Of Enoch [Describes / Says / Reveals] [familiar thing] — And [withheld specific]**

The clause after the em dash is the entire hook. What separates the top from the bottom is
what that clause promises:

| Views | Second clause | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1.7M | "And **Why** God Sealed It There" | a **motive** |
| 393k | "And The **Sins** That Actually Send You There" | a list that **implicates the viewer** |
| 281k | "And Where **They** Actually End Up" | implicates the viewer |
| 95k | "And Why It Is Sealed" | subject too narrow (Grand Canyon) |
| 75k | "And What Happens When One Falls" | abstract, no personal stake |

**Motive beats description. Implication beats spectacle.** And the biggest performer takes
something everyone already knows exists — the ocean floor — rather than something exotic.

Every title below is built to that rule.

## 1. The Watchers' curriculum — *recommended second*

> ### The Book Of Enoch Names Every Angel Who Taught Humanity — And Why The Teaching Itself Was The Crime

**The passage:** 1 Enoch 8. Not a vague charge — a syllabus, with names attached.

- **Azazel** — swords, knives, shields, breastplates; the metals of the earth and the art of
  working them; bracelets, ornaments, antimony, the beautifying of the eyelids, costly
  stones and colouring tinctures
- **Semjaza** — enchantments and root-cuttings
- **Armaros** — the resolving of enchantments
- **Baraqijal** — astrology
- **Kokabel** — the constellations
- **Ezeqeel** — the knowledge of the clouds
- **Araqiel** — the signs of the earth
- **Shamsiel** — the signs of the sun
- **Sariel** — the course of the moon

**The withheld reveal (final act):** the indictment is not sorcery. Read the list again —
metallurgy, cosmetics, astronomy, meteorology. It is a list of the foundations of
civilisation. The crime was not what was taught. It was that it arrived early.

**Cold-open object:** a bronze blade, or a jar of antimony eye-paint from a museum case —
something the viewer has seen and thought nothing of.

**Why it works:** the second clause promises a motive, and the list implicates the viewer —
they used four things off it before breakfast.

## 2. Where demons come from — *recommended first test*

> ### The Book Of Enoch Says Demons Are Not Fallen Angels — And Describes What They Actually Are

**The passage:** 1 Enoch 15–16. "Evil spirits have proceeded from their bodies; because they
are born from men and from the holy Watchers is their beginning and primal origin." The
spirits of heaven dwell in heaven; the spirits born on earth dwell on earth. They "afflict,
oppress, destroy, attack, do battle" — and they "take no food, but nevertheless hunger and
thirst."

**The withheld reveal (final act):** a demon, in this account, is not an angel at all. It is
the disembodied spirit of a dead giant — half-human by origin, permanently hungry, unable to
eat, and explicitly *not* judged until the end. Which means, in the text's own terms, they
were never removed.

**Cold-open object:** the Qumran fragment glass itself, or a single line of Aramaic.

**Why it's the first test:** it runs the channel's two highest-performing formulas at once —
"is not what the church teaches" (393k and 257k both used it) and a claim that implicates the
viewer's present. And unlike a planet episode, it is genuinely what the text says, so the
ledger and the real passage carry it without strain.

## 3. The petition that was refused

> ### The Book Of Enoch Says Fallen Angels Begged A Human To Plead For Them — And Records The Answer He Brought Back

**The passage:** 1 Enoch 13–14. The Watchers ask Enoch to write out their petition for
forgiveness and length of days. He sits at the waters of Dan, south-west of Hermon, reads it
until he sleeps, and receives the answer in a vision. He returns to find them gathered and
weeping at Abelsjail, between Lebanon and Senir, faces covered.

**The withheld reveal (final act):** the answer, delivered by a man to angels — "your
petition will not be granted unto you throughout all the days of eternity" — and worse, the
sentence attached: "from henceforth you shall not ascend into heaven unto all eternity."
There was one intercession, it was carried by a human being, and it failed.

**Cold-open object:** the place. Mount Hermon, photographed as it is now.

**Why it works:** it is a story with a beginning and an end, which none of the reference
channel's episodes are. Named locations give the act cycle four real physical anchors.

## 4. The oath holding creation together

> ### The Book Of Enoch Describes The Oath That Holds The Universe Together — And Names The Angel Who Gave It Away

**The passage:** 1 Enoch 69. Kesbeel — "his name is Beqa" — is chief of the oath. He asks
Michael to show him the secret name so it might be spoken in the oath. Through that oath
heaven was suspended before the world was created; the earth was founded upon water; the sea
was given sand as its boundary, "and it does not go beyond it." The power of the oath, called
Akae, is placed in Michael's charge.

**The withheld reveal (final act):** creation is not described as held together by power. It
is held by a spoken oath — an agreement, which implies parties, which implies it could have
gone otherwise. And the being who obtained the name that makes it binding did so by asking.

**Cold-open object:** a shoreline. The sea stopping at sand, which the text names as the
oath's visible proof.

**Why it works:** "names the angel who gave it away" is a motive-and-culprit promise. It is
also the most cinematic of the five.

## 5. The calendar that was replaced — *the safest*

> ### The Book Of Enoch Describes A Different Calendar — And Why Keeping The Wrong One Was Treated As A Sin

**The passage:** 1 Enoch 72–82, the Book of the Heavenly Luminaries, revealed to Enoch by
the angel Uriel. A 364-day year: four seasons of ninety-one days, twelve months of thirty,
four seasonal days. Exactly fifty-two weeks.

**The withheld reveal (final act):** this is not a curiosity. The community at Qumran — the
same community whose caves produced the eleven Enoch manuscripts — kept this calendar in
direct opposition to the Temple in Jerusalem, and treated keeping the wrong date as sin.
And the arithmetic does not close: a 364-day year loses more than a day annually against the
true solar year, and scholars still dispute how, or whether, they corrected it.

**Cold-open object:** fragment 4Q208 — an actual astronomical Enoch manuscript, and already
on your verified list.

**Why it's the safest:** it is the least sensational and by far the best sourced. If you want
one episode that could survive a hostile fact-check line by line, this is it. It also has the
strongest fact-ledger match of the five.

## Which facts carry each episode

Beyond the three every-episode items (Laodicea, the Qumran discoveries, the eleven
manuscripts):

| Episode | Strongest ledger support |
|---|---|
| 1. Watchers' curriculum | Milik's Aramaic edition; Nickelsburg's commentary (covers 1–36) |
| 2. Demons | Nickelsburg's commentary (covers 1–36); Knibb; Ephraim Isaac |
| 3. Petition | Nickelsburg's commentary (covers 1–36); Milik; 4Q201, 4Q204 |
| 4. The oath | **1 Enoch 2 (chs 37–82)**; Matthew Black's edition |
| 5. Calendar | **1 Enoch 2**; **Neugebauer's appendix on chs 72–82**; **4Q208–4Q211**; Drawnel |

Episodes 4 and 5 have the tightest matches — the ledger holds sources that cover exactly
those chapters. Episode 5 in particular can name a manuscript that *is* the subject.

## Suggested order

**Test with #2 (demons).** Closest to the channel's proven formula, genuinely in the text,
and a reveal that lands. If the pipeline can write that one well, it can write the rest.

Then **#1**, then **#5**, then **#3**, then **#4**.

## One honest note

All five are grounded in what 1 Enoch actually says, which is a deliberate change from the
reference channel's approach — its Mars, Saturn, Moon and Grand Canyon episodes are not in
Enoch at all. That is a real trade: those invented-subject episodes are its top performers.
Grounded episodes are more defensible and more durable; whether they pull the same numbers
on this channel is exactly what these five are for.
