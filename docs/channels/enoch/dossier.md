## Which Enoch book is which

Getting this wrong is the easiest way to get a video torn apart in the comments, because the
three books are routinely mixed together online — including by the reference channel.

| | **1 Enoch** | **2 Enoch** | **3 Enoch** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Also called | Ethiopic Enoch | Slavonic Enoch; Secrets of Enoch | Hebrew Enoch; Sefer Hekhalot |
| Survives in | Ge'ez (complete) | Old Church Slavonic; Coptic fragments in Nubia | Hebrew |
| Length | 108 chapters, 5 sections | ~68 chapters, long + short recensions | Hekhalot/merkabah text |
| At Qumran? | Yes — ~11 fragmentary Aramaic copies | No | No |
| In any canon? | Yes — Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo | **No. None.** Not even Ethiopian | No |
| Heavens | **One** (ch. 14, the crystal house) | **Seven** (ten in the longer recension) | Seven palaces |

**The seven heavens are 2 Enoch, not 1 Enoch.** Any script that walks through seven heavens
while citing Qumran fragments, Ge'ez manuscripts or Knibb's 1978 edition has welded two
different books together. The first generated "Seven Heavens" script did exactly this.

Sections of 1 Enoch: Watchers (1-36), Parables (37-71), Luminaries (72-82), Dream Visions
(83-90), Epistle (91-108).

### Trap: the 366 books are not real

2 Enoch 23 has Enoch write **366 books** (360 in Recension A) at Vretil's dictation over
thirty days and nights. That is a **plot point inside the story**, not a surviving library.
Three Enoch books survive. Never let a script imply otherwise.

### Trap: Laodicea did not ban Enoch

Canon 60 of Laodicea (364) is of disputed authenticity and did not name Enoch. Do not say
"Laodicea banned the Book of Enoch."

## Verified material

**2 Enoch**
- Seven heavens (ten in the longer recension); ascent guided by two men/angels.
- 2nd heaven: a prison. Angels in darkness, weeping, awaiting judgment; they see Enoch passing
  and ask him to pray for them.
- 3rd heaven: paradise *and* a place of torment, in the same layer.
- 5th heaven: the Grigori (Watchers).
- **Vretil** — archangel scribe, 2 Enoch 22:10-11 and 23. "Swiftest of the archangels in
  wisdom"; records all the Lord's deeds. God tells him to bring the books out of the
  storehouses and give Enoch **a pen for speed-writing**. Dictates 30 days and 30 nights
  without stopping. Name unstable across manuscripts: **Vretil / Vrevoil / Vereveil / Pravuil**.
- First English translation: **R.H. Charles & W.R. Morfill, 1896**.
- Coptic fragments found in Nubia — ends the "medieval Slavic forgery" reading.
  *Not verified against the excavation report. Check before it carries a video.*

**1 Enoch**
- Ch. 14 — house of crystal, floor of crystal, ceiling like the path of the stars.
- Ch. 81:1-2 — "Observe, Enoch, these heavenly tablets, and read what is written thereon, and
  mark every individual fact." He reads the deeds of all mankind **"to the remotest
  generations"** — the record already contains the unborn.
- Ch. 71 — Enoch himself is called **Son of Man**. Real, and explosive.
- Ch. 18:13-16 and 21:3-6 — seven stars bound at the edge of creation for ten thousand years,
  punished because "they did not come forth at their appointed times." The Greek root of
  *planet* means *wanderer*; there were exactly seven classical wandering stars.
- Dead Sea Scrolls publication monopoly: ~40-year embargo; Milik held the Enoch fragments 29
  years; broken September 1991 by the Huntington Library.
