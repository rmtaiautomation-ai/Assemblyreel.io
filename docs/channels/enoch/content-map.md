# Content Map — Enoch: The Top 40

The launch slate, ranked. Read with `channel-playbook.md` (format) and `dossiers/enoch.md`
(facts). `video-slate-enoch.md` holds deep treatments of five of these.

**Make them in this order.** The ranking is built for a cold channel: the earliest videos ask
the questions the most people already have, and every one of them is real text.

## What each entry is ranked on

1. **Does a normal person already have this question?** — "what happens when I die" beats
   "what is the Apocalypse of Weeks", always.
2. **Is it frightening?**
3. **Does it make them say "wait, is that real?"**
4. **Can we answer that with a chapter number?**

## Title style

Subject **plain and up front**, twist withheld. The viewer must know what the video is about
within four words. Clever construction loses to a clear noun.

> **The Book Of Enoch [Describes / Says] [familiar thing] — And [withheld specific]**

## The rule that protects the whole channel

**Describe. Never claim.** The text is strange enough that the viewer draws the conclusion
themselves. The moment narration says "spaceship", "alien" or "UFO", the channel owns a claim
it cannot defend. Say what is written; let the comments say the rest. That argument is free
reach.

## Avoid — already owned by the reference channel

Ocean floor, hell, heaven, Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, the Moon, the cremated, the stars, life on
other planets, seven levels beneath the earth, the Grand Canyon, Jesus, what God made before
humans.

# TIER 1 — Launch with these (1–10)

Universal questions, maximum fear, every one verified.

### 1. The Book Of Enoch Describes What Happens The Moment You Die — And Where You Are Taken First
`1 Enoch 22` · **Verified**
Not heaven. Not hell. A mountain with **hollow places** cut into it, and the dead sorted into
separate chambers by category — before any judgment happens. You are filed on arrival, and you
wait there. *The only question every single viewer has already asked themselves.*

### 2. The Book Of Enoch Describes What God Looks Like — And Why No Angel Is Allowed To See Him
`1 Enoch 14:18-25` · **Verified**
A crystal throne, wheels like the shining sun, streams of flaming fire pouring from underneath.
"His raiment shone more brightly than the sun and was whiter than any snow." Then: **"None of
the angels could enter and could behold His face... and no flesh could behold Him."**
A human is standing in a room angels are not permitted to enter.

### 3. The Book Of Enoch Describes What Demons Actually Are — And Why They Are Always Hungry
`1 Enoch 15-16` · **Verified**
Not fallen angels. The spirits of **dead giants**. They "take no food, but nevertheless hunger
and thirst" — permanently starving, permanently unable to eat. And they remain on earth **until
the day of the great judgment.** Which means, in this account, they are still here.

### 4. The Book Of Enoch Describes Who Really Runs The World — And What Is Waiting For Them
`1 Enoch 46, 62-63` · **Verified**
"The kings and the mighty who possess the earth." They deny the Lord of Spirits. At the end
they beg for mercy and are refused — "none shall seek for themselves mercy, for their life is
at an end" — and are handed to the righteous. *The viewer supplies the modern names.*

### 5. The Book Of Enoch Says Your Life Was Written Down Before You Lived It — And Names Who Holds The Book
`1 Enoch 81` + `2 Enoch 22-23` · **Verified**
"Observe, Enoch, these heavenly tablets, and read what is written thereon." He reads the deeds
of all mankind **"to the remotest generations"** — people not yet born. In 2 Enoch the archangel
**Vretil** holds the books, hands Enoch a pen made for speed-writing, and dictates thirty days
and thirty nights without stopping.

### 6. The Book Of Enoch Describes Noah's Birth — And Why His Own Father Thought He Was Not Human
`1 Enoch 106-107` · **Verified**
White hair. Skin white as snow. Eyes that light up the whole house. Lamech is so frightened of
his newborn son he becomes convinced the child is a Watcher's, and travels to find Enoch to ask.
*Opens like a horror film. The baby is Noah.*

### 7. The Book Of Enoch Says Abel Is Still Screaming — And Names The Only Thing That Will Stop Him
`1 Enoch 22` · **Verified**
Raphael tells Enoch the voice is **Abel**, murdered by Cain, and that it "went forth to heaven
crying unceasingly." He is still making his case, and will not stop until Cain's bloodline is
erased from the earth.

### 8. The Book Of Enoch Describes The Giants — And What They Did When The Food Ran Out
`1 Enoch 7` · **Verified**
Crops. Then men. Then each other. Then birds, beasts, reptiles, fish. Then they begin drinking
blood. A food chain worked downward in order.

### 9. The Book Of Enoch Describes The Punishment Of The Fallen Angels — And They Were Made To Watch
`1 Enoch 10` · **Verified**
God orders Gabriel to set the giants against each other: "let them perish by mutual slaughter."
The Watchers are bound for seventy generations **"when their sons have slain one another, and
they have seen the destruction of their beloved ones."** They watch first. Then they are buried.

### 10. The Book Of Enoch Describes How Enoch Was Taken Up — And What He Saw Inside
`1 Enoch 14:8-17` · **Verified**
Clouds and mist call him. Winds rush him upward. An outer wall of hailstones ringed with tongues
of fire. Inside: a floor of fire, a ceiling of lightning and the paths of the stars, walls of
crystal — **hot as fire and cold as ice at the same time.** Then a second, larger structure
within it. *Describe it exactly. Say nothing more.*

# TIER 2 — Build on the audience (11–20)

### 11. The Book Of Enoch Describes What Happens To People Who Got Away With It
`1 Enoch 22:12-13` · *Needs chapter check*
A separate chamber for the ones who were never punished in life. The text divides the dead by
whether justice already reached them.

### 12. The Book Of Enoch Names Every Angel Who Taught Humanity — And Why The Teaching Was The Crime
`1 Enoch 8` · **Verified**
A syllabus with names attached. Azazel: weapons and cosmetics, in the same breath. Semjaza:
enchantments and root-cuttings. Baraqijal: astrology.

### 13. The Book Of Enoch Describes Azazel's Sentence — And Why His Face Had To Be Covered
`1 Enoch 10` · **Verified**
Bound hand and foot. Cast into darkness at Dudael. Covered with rough jagged rocks. Face covered
*that he may not see light.* Someone designed this.

### 14. The Book Of Enoch Describes The Oath 200 Angels Swore — And Why Their Leader Was Afraid
`1 Enoch 6` · **Verified**
Semjaza makes them bind themselves by mutual curse *first*, because he fears he alone will pay
the penalty. The rebellion opens with its leader protecting himself.

### 15. The Book Of Enoch Says A Man Asked God To Forgive The Fallen Angels — And Records The Answer
`1 Enoch 12-14` · **Verified**
The angels are too frightened to ask themselves, so they send a human. The refusal is handed to
Enoch to read back to them aloud, to their faces.

### 16. There Is A Second Book Of Enoch — And No Church On Earth Accepted It
`2 Enoch` · **Verified**
Not Jewish, not Catholic, not Protestant — and not even Ethiopian, which kept the first one.
Survives in Old Church Slavonic. Nobody's Bible.

### 17. The Second Book Of Enoch Says There Is A Prison Inside Heaven — And Names Who Is Locked In It
`2 Enoch 7` · **Verified**
The second heaven. Angels in darkness, weeping, awaiting a judgment that has not come. They see
Enoch passing and beg him to pray for them.

### 18. The Book Of Enoch Says Seven Stars Were Arrested — And Describes The Prison They Are Bound In
`1 Enoch 18:13-16, 21:3-6` · **Verified**
Bound for ten thousand years, punished because "they did not come forth at their appointed
times." The Greek root of *planet* means *wanderer*, and there were seven classical wanderers.

### 19. The Book Of Enoch Names All Seven Archangels — And The One Job Each Was Given
`1 Enoch 20` · **Verified**
Uriel, Raphael, Raguel, Michael, Saraqael, Gabriel, Remiel. Most people can name two. Each has
a defined portfolio.

### 20. The Book Of Enoch Describes How To Actually Get In — And It Is Not What You Were Taught
`1 Enoch 104` · *Needs chapter check*
The promises made to the righteous, and the conditions attached. The practical answer to the
question tier 1 opens.

# TIER 4 — The suppression season (31–40)

The manuscripts themselves as subject. Run once the audience is invested.

DSS publication monopoly · **Verified** · Milik and the Aramaic Enoch fragments. Broken

### 32. Everyone Quotes The Book Of Enoch — And Almost Nobody Notices It Is In The Bible
`Jude 14-15` · **Verified** · Jude quotes 1 Enoch 1:9 directly, by name, as prophecy. It is in
every Bible sold today.

### 33. The Book Of Enoch Survived In One Country — And Nobody In Europe Could Read It For 1,000 Years
Ge'ez transmission · **Verified** · Ethiopia kept it whole while the West lost it entirely.

### 34. The Angel Who Holds The Record Of Every Human Life Has Four Different Names
`2 Enoch 22` · **Verified** · Vretil / Vrevoil / Vereveil / Pravuil. The scribe of all deeds
could not keep his own name intact through copying.

### 35. The Book Of Enoch Describes Twelve Gates The Wind Comes Through — And What Each One Brings
`1 Enoch 33-36, 76` · *Needs chapter check* · Named portals, assigned effects.

### 36. The Book Of Enoch Describes Seven Mountains Of Precious Stone — And The Tree Nobody May Touch

### 37. The Book Of Enoch Says The Earth Will Give Back What Was Entrusted To It

### 38. The Book Of Enoch Describes A Valley Where The Cursed Are Gathered — And Who Watches

### 39. The Book Of Enoch Says The Son Of Man Was Named Before The Sun Existed
`1 Enoch 48` · *Needs chapter check* · Named before the stars were made, and hidden until needed.

### 40. The Book Of Enoch Was Written By Someone Who Was Not Enoch — And That Makes It Stranger

the story.

## The honest trade

The reference channel's top performers — Mars, Saturn, the Grand Canyon — are **not in Enoch at
all.** Ours are. That may cost early numbers.

The resolution is Tier 1. A baby born with eyes that light a room. Giants working down a food
chain until they eat each other. A murder victim who has never stopped screaming. **We do not
need to invent, because the real text is stranger than the inventions.** An invented Mars episode
dies the day someone checks. These do not.
