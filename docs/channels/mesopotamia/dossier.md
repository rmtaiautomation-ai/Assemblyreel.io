## The trap that would sink this channel

Almost everything online about the Anunnaki traces to **Zecharia Sitchin, *The 12th Planet*
(1976)**. Assyriologists reject it wholesale. If a script repeats Sitchin as fact, the video
is indefensible the moment anyone who reads cuneiform watches it.

| Popular claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| Nibiru is a hidden 12th planet on a 3,600-year orbit | **Sitchin's invention.** In MUL.APIN, *Nibiru* is **Jupiter** — "it keeps changing its position and crosses the sky." Crossing = Jupiter's movement, not crossing other planets. Benno Landsberger treated the term scholarly by 1961. |

**Rule for this dossier: never say "alien," "extraterrestrial," "spacecraft" or "Nibiru
planet" as fact.** The channel's sourcing rule already bans invention; this is where it will

## Verified material

**Atrahasis** — Akkadian, standard Old Babylonian form **c. 1800-1700 BC**. Oldest known copy
carries a colophon from the reign of **Ammi-Saduqa of Babylon (c. 1646-1626 BC)**. The
earliest fully preserved account of both human creation and a cataclysmic flood.

- The **Igigi**, the lower order of gods, dig the canals and maintain the irrigation for the
  senior gods, the **Anunnaki**.
- After **forty years** of it, the Igigi **revolt and refuse to continue**. This is, in effect,
  the first recorded labour strike in literature.
- The divine assembly's solution: kill one of their own — a god named **We-ila**, described as
  "one who had a mind in him" — and knead his flesh and blood into river clay.
- Humanity is made from that mixture, for one stated purpose: **to take over the work the gods
  refused to do.**

That is the real text. Humans manufactured as a labour force, from clay and the blood of an
executed god, because the workers above them went on strike.

**Related texts to mine**
- *Enuma Elish* — Marduk makes humans from Kingu's blood to free the gods from labour.
- *Enki and Ninmah* — Ninmah makes flawed humans; a divine contest over defective creations.
- *Sumerian King List* — implausibly long antediluvian reigns, and a flood dividing the list.
- *Descent of Inanna*; *Adapa*; *Etana*; *Epic of Gilgamesh* (flood tablet XI).
- *MUL.APIN* — the actual Babylonian astronomical compendium.
